# Chore Rush!

A 90s-style platformer: finish your chores before your parents get home. 13 levels (Isaac's room, Annie's room, kitchen, living room, backyard, garage, laundry room, bathroom, home office, music room, basement, attic, whole house), each with its own music genre.

Open `index.html` in any browser (single self-contained file). Arrows/WASD to move, Space to jump, M mute, P pause. Touch controls on phones.
