# Chore Rush!

A 90s-style platformer: finish your chores before your parents get home. 10 levels (bedroom, kitchen, living room, backyard, garage, laundry room, bathroom, basement, attic, whole house), each with its own music genre.

Open `index.html` in any browser (single self-contained file). Arrows/WASD to move, Space to jump, M mute, P pause. Touch controls on phones.
